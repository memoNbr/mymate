# NOTES — how I like to be taught

- **Medium depth.** Skip heavy math; keep formulas to one line with a plain-English gloss.
- **Show the real code.** Prefer the files that actually exist in
  `cognitive-agent-literature/tools/cogarch_bootstrap.py` and `docs/cognitive-agents.html`.
- **Examples over definitions.** A tiny runnable loop beats a paragraph.
- **Short lessons.** One page, one clear win; I'll come back for references later.
- English is fine (my casual messages may be loosely formatted — no need to mirror that).
- I like a quiz at the end to check the win stuck (equal-length answers so I can't guess by length).
- Future lessons should reuse the shared stylesheet in `assets/`.
